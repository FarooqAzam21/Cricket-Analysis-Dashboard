## 📊 What Each Type Shows

### Batsman
- **Focus**: Batting performance
- **Key Stats**: Runs, Average, Strike Rate, Centuries, Fifties
- **Use**: Predict batting contribution

### All-Rounder
- **Focus**: Overall performance (bat + ball)
- **Key Stats**: Runs, Wickets, Average, Bowl Avg, Economy
- **Use**: Complete player value assessment

### Bowler
- **Focus**: Bowling performance + lower-order batting
- **Key Stats**: Runs, Wickets, Bowling Average, Economy
- **Use**: Tail-end batting potential

### Wicket-Keeper
- **Focus**: Keeping + batting combination
- **Key Stats**: Runs, Average, Strike Rate, Matches
- **Use**: Keeper-batsman performance
