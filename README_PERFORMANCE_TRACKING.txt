📊 FANTASY POINTS FORMULA:

BATSMAN EXAMPLE (45 runs, 35 balls, 5 fours, 1 six):
   45 runs × 1         = 45 points
   5 fours × 1         = 5 points
   1 six × 2           = 2 points
   SR 128.6% > 120%    = 5 points
   ──────────────────────────────
   TOTAL               = 57 points

BOWLER EXAMPLE (2 wickets, economy 6.5):
   2 wickets × 25      = 50 points
   Economy 6.5 (6-8)   = 5 points
   ──────────────────────────────
   TOTAL               = 55 points
